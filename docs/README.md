# Documentation

This directory preserves the repository's document corpus. The classification
is structural, not a scientific validation of the content: documents in the
active areas still need to be reviewed against the findings from later
studies.

## Structure

### `theory/`

General formulations and explanations of DOFT:

- [`MANIFESTO_v1.8.md`](./theory/MANIFESTO_v1.8.md)
- [`MANIFESTO_EXPLAINED.md`](./theory/MANIFESTO_EXPLAINED.md)
- [`MANIFESTO_EXPLICADO.md`](./theory/MANIFESTO_EXPLICADO.md) — intentional
  Spanish edition.
- [`APPENDIX_Nuclear_to_Electronic_Locking.md`](./theory/APPENDIX_Nuclear_to_Electronic_Locking.md)

### `studies/`

Index of studies and documents preserved here:

- [`studies/README.md`](./studies/) — incorporation status for Study 01–06.
- [`studies/study-01/`](./studies/study-01/) — extracted overview, findings,
  and research log for Study 01.
- [`studies/study-02/`](./studies/study-02/) — extracted overview, findings,
  and research log for Study 02, including the published Study 03 crossover.
- [`studies/study-05/`](./studies/study-05/) — extracted overview, findings,
  and research log for the hierarchical oscillator-network study.

No standalone conclusions are retained from Study 03 or Study 04, following
the author's direction. Their status is recorded in the study index; the
Study 03 crossover that appears inside the Study 02 publication remains
audited there for provenance. Study 06 is outside the current extraction
scope.

### `archive/`

Historical material preserved for context and traceability:

- [`early-versions/`](./archive/early-versions/) — earlier manifesto and
  introductory-document versions.
- [`protocols/`](./archive/protocols/) — protocols associated with the
  deprecated simulator.
- [`study-01-early-theory/`](./archive/study-01-early-theory/) — the earlier,
  broader Study 01 theory report, superseded as the active findings summary.

Nothing in `archive/` should automatically be interpreted as a current
specification.
