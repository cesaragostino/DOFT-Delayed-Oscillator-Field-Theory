# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in the DOFT repository should be respectful, evidence-focused,
and constructive.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing ideas and evidence without attacking people;
- distinguishing uncertainty, criticism, and falsification from hostility;
- giving proper credit for research, code, data, and review work;
- respecting privacy, consent, and publication restrictions;
- accepting correction when a claim or implementation is shown to be wrong;
- helping keep the research record understandable to future readers.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
misrepresentation of evidence, plagiarism, exposure of private information,
or disruption intended to prevent good-faith collaboration.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other public or private project spaces controlled by the maintainer.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/cesaragostino).
Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict
participation, or take other proportionate action. Reports will be considered
in context and handled as privately as practical.

Anyone making an enforcement decision should disclose a conflict of interest
and, when possible, seek a neutral reviewer.
